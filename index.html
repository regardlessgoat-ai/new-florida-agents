Questions

Straight answers

What is in each row?

One new agent per row: name, mailing address, city, county, zip code, licence number and licence date. It is ready for labels and mail merge.

Which counties are covered?

All 67 Florida counties. This week's file has 521 new agents in 44 counties. Dade, Broward and Palm Beach have the most.

How fresh is the list?

Every agent in the file got a Florida sales associate licence in the last 14 days and has no broker on record. A new file is made each week, so you always work with this week's new licences.

When and how does it arrive?

By email, as one CSV file. Your first file comes within three working days of your order. After that you get one file each week.

Should I take one county or all of Florida?

One county gives you every new agent in the county you choose. It suits a firm that recruits around one office. All of Florida gives you every county in one file. It suits a firm with several offices, or one that recruits across the state.

How fast can I start recruiting?

The day your file arrives. Open it in Excel or your mail-merge tool, print the labels, and post your letters. Most agents choose a firm in their first weeks, so an early letter counts.

Are there rules for writing to these agents?

Yes. A licensee who advertises must follow the state advertising rule, 61J2-10.025: show your firm's licensed name and say nothing false. Any letters or emails you send are your own responsibility.

How do I cancel?

Write to newfloridaagents@gmail.com. The plan stops and no further month is charged.
